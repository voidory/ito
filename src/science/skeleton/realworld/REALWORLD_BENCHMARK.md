# FEAT-005B — Real-World Carbon-Skeleton Benchmark

Auto-generated by `report.test.ts` (run with `ITO_WRITE_REALWORLD_REPORT=1`). Single source of truth: `realworld/corpus.ts` → `manifest.json` → Pillow renderer (`scripts/gen-realworld-corpus.py`) → `png/*.png` + `variants.json`.

## What this benchmark is — and is not

- **Separate category.** These are REAL named organic structures reduced to their carbon skeletons and scored on their own. Their numbers are **never combined** with the synthetic SVG fixture benchmark (`../BENCHMARK.md`).
- **Rendered, not scanned.** Each image is a genuine anti-aliased PNG produced by a raster engine (Pillow, 4× supersampling) with real-diagram artefacts: anti-aliasing, varied line weights, element/group labels placed next to or touching bonds, non-120° angles, a range of resolutions, and (on the held-out set) rotation, skew, lossy compression, Gaussian / salt-and-pepper noise, reduced resolution and reduced contrast.
- **Honest limitation.** Rendered structures are NOT photographs of scanned textbook pages or hand-drawn diagrams. They exercise the *actual* PNG-decode path and the real-diagram artefacts the 1px SVG fixtures lack, but photographed-page evaluation remains the final validation step before automatic recognition is trusted in the Identify workflow.

- **Dev/eval split honoured.** The corpus is partitioned deterministically (stratified by category) into a development set and a held-out evaluation set. The algorithm was **not** tuned against the held-out set; robustness transforms are applied only to the held-out set.
